The problemWhat Kinlock doesWhat you get
The problem

Your child is on WhatsApp or games far longer than you agreed.

What Kinlock does

You give each app its own daily limit. When the time is used up, the app stops.

What you get

Screen time you chose, not screen time you argued about.

The problem

The phone is still in use late at night.

What Kinlock does

You set a fixed timetable, for example off after 20:00. The clock wins even if daily time is left.

What you get

A child who is not on the phone at bedtime.

The problem

Every limit turns into a fight at the door.

What Kinlock does

Your child sends a request from their phone. It arrives on yours with the reason. You approve, refuse, or change the time.

What you get

The decision is yours, and it takes seconds.

The problem

You cannot trust the phone to follow your rules while you are out.

What Kinlock does

The child's phone checks with your phone before it does restricted things. You scan one QR code to link them.

What you get

Control from your own phone, wherever you are.
